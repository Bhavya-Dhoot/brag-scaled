# Jaipur Block Print

Hand-block-printed textiles and Rajputana architecture: the launch printed onto cloth one stamp at a time.

- **Best for:** consumer, fashion and craft brands, D2C, anything tactile or handmade
- **Palette:** Jaipur pink `#e07a7a`, indigo `#2b3a67`, *dabu* sand `#e8d8b8`, madder red `#a4302b`, marigold `#f0a202`
- **Type:** Yatra One for display (a Devanagari-inspired Latin face), Karla for body
- **Texture:** cotton weave, slight ink bleed, the repeat of a carved wooden block, *jaali* lattice screens
- **Signature move:** a carved wooden block stamps down, lifts, and leaves the motif. The pattern builds stamp by stamp until it frames the content, with a slight misalignment between repeats as proof it's handmade.
- **Transitions:** a fabric bolt unrolls, or a *jaali* screen slides across
- **Sound:** a wooden block thud (tuned to the key), dholak rhythm, bansuri flute melody, anklet bells on accents
- **Watch out:** too many motifs reads as wallpaper. Keep one motif per scene.
