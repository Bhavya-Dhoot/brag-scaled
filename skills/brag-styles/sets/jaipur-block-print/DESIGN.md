## Brand & Style
The design system channels the heritage of Jaipur block-printing (Bagru and Sanganer traditions) and the monumental intimacy of Rajputana palace architecture. It targets cinematic art directors, luxury brand storytellers, and visual sequence designers crafting 16:9 widescreen animatics and product unveilings. 

The aesthetic is Tactile & Artisanal: raw unbleached cotton slub grain, physical woodblock press marks, visible ink overprints with slight organic misregistration, scalloped jharokha silhouettes, and precise geometric jaali (lattice) matrices. High-tech digital gradients, glossy finishes, and generic vector gloss are strictly excluded. Modernity emerges through structural discipline: severe 16:9 cinematic framing counterbalanced by the warmth of natural dye pigments, hand-carved teak contours, running basting stitches, and bespoke ornamental regalia synthesized from the alphanumeric geometry of 'NX' and 'NEXUS'. The resulting atmosphere is regal, grounded, tactile, and ceremonial.

## Colors
The palette is derived from historical plant and mineral dyes applied to unbleached cotton canvas:
- **Primary (`#a4302b` - Madder Red / Manjistha):** Used for focal insignia, key narrative callouts, stamped authentication seals, and prominent storyboard timeline anchors.
- **Secondary (`#2b3a67` - Dabu Indigo):** The grounding architectural ink. Applied to structural jharokha framing, complex jaali vectors, narrative subtitles, and primary action scripts.
- **Tertiary (`#f0a202` - Marigold / Genda Phool):** An accent of royal celebration, highlighting camera movements, sound-effect markers, focus targets, and stitch highlights.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#e8d8b8` - Cotton Fabric Sand / Kora):** The warm, textural base simulating raw unbleached khadi cotton cloth.
- **Supporting Accents:** 
  - **Jaipur Terracotta Pink (`#e07a7a`):** Wash tone for structural panel backings and secondary woodblock stamps.
  - **White Lime Plaster / Chuna (`#fcfbf7`):** Clean negative space, unprinted textile reserves, and illuminated storyboard frames.
  - **Carved Dark Teak Wood (`#3e2723`):** High-contrast linework, frame borders, typography headings, and technical storyboard metadata.

Color applications must avoid smooth digital blending. Overlapping colors should simulate physical printing via multiply blends or hard-edge mechanical overprints, preserving the slight misregistration of hand-carved blocks.

## Typography
Typography is balanced between the heavy, Devanagari-inflected Latin letterforms of Yatra One and the clean, functional legibility of Karla.

- **Display & Sequence Titles (Yatra One):** Delivers artisanal dignity with distinctive top-hanging visual weights, angular terminals, and handcrafted charm. Used for sequence numbers, chapter titles, scene identifiers, and product title cards.
- **Narrative & Directional Text (Karla):** Chosen for its grotesque quirks and open spacing, handling shot descriptions, camera movement directives, dynamic transitions, and duration readouts.
- **Letter Spacing & Casing:** Labels and technical metadata (Timecode, Focal Length, Camera Rig) use uppercase Karla with expanded tracking (0.1em to 0.15em) to replicate the stamped look of archival royal blueprints and inventory registers.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a strict cinematic 16:9 widescreen canvas logic. All primary viewport presentations maintain a 16:9 aspect ratio container (`aspect-video`), nested within deliberate margins that echo the mounting borders of miniature Rajput paintings.

- **Grid Framework:** A 12-column modular grid with generous 1.5rem (`24px`) gutters accommodates widescreen shot-planning arrays: 1-up master animatic view, 2-up comparison frames, 3-up triptychs, or 6-up contact sheets.
- **Outer Canvas Margins:** Anchored at 2.5rem (`40px`), expanding to 3.5rem on ultra-wide screens to allow for architectural framing devices—scalloped margins, running stitch tracks, and stamped scene registries.
- **Mobile Adaptations:** Collapses to a 4-column flow with 1rem margins. Storyboard cells preserve their 16:9 ratio and reflow vertically, with directional notes pinned below rather than laterally.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in this system rejects simulated digital drop shadows and blur layers. Instead, depth is articulated through physical relief, surface layering, and block-print density:

- **Debossed Teak Relief:** Cards and frames are carved into the neutral cotton plane using crisp, 1.5px to 2px inner borders (`#3e2723` at 40% opacity) and tactile offset silhouettes (`2px 2px 0px #3e2723`), echoing the impression of a wooden printing block driven firmly into wet fiber.
- **Tonal Fiber Stacking:** Layer 0 is raw unbleached cloth (`#e8d8b8`). Layer 1 is pressed lime plaster matting (`#fcfbf7`). Layer 2 consists of architectural jharokha silhouettes or indigo panels (`#2b3a67`). Layer 3 holds stamped madder-red insignia, shot annotations, and canvas tags.
- **The Ink Bleed & Misalignment:** Visual hierarchy is accented by deliberate mechanical shifts. Overprint outlines in Madder Red or Indigo offset 1px to 1.5px from their solid fill coordinates, creating the tangible imperfection of a manual second-strike impression.

## Shapes
Geometry is rooted in the cusped, multi-lobed jharokha arch and octagonal jaali work of Amber and City Palace Jaipur, combined with crisp structural cuts:

- **Soft Contours (`roundedness: 1`):** Structural boundaries adopt subtle 0.25rem rounding to evoke soft, woven textile borders rather than cold machined edges.
- **Rajputana Cusped Arches:** Frame caps, modal headers, and key sequence focal windows feature five-lobed or nine-lobed scalloped profiles.
- **Tailored Fabric Cuts:** Storyboard metadata badges and status pills use chamfered 45-degree corner notches or clipped ribbon ends, simulating sewn tailor-swatch labels.
- **Motif Geometry:** Buta (paisley flame) medallions and ornamental corner brackets are constructed purely from geometric deconstructions of the letters 'N' and 'X', interlaced with traditional trellis symmetry.

## Components

### 16:9 Storyboard Frame
The core component of the animatic suite.
- **Structure:** 16:9 ratio enclosure bordered by a triple-line frame: an outer 2px carved teak stroke (`#3e2723`), a 4px unprinted sand gap, and an inner 1px running dash (`#a4302b` stitched thread pattern).
- **Header Crest:** A centered scalloped jharokha arch housing the Scene / Shot ID (e.g., `SCENE 04 • SHOT B`) in Yatra One.
- **Footer Shelf:** An integrated woodblock rail displaying camera parameters (Lens: `35mm Anamorphic`, Motion: `Slow Dolly In`, Timing: `00:04:12`).

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary Action (The Royal Seal):** Solid madder red (`#a4302b`) ground with chuna white (`#fcfbf7`) Yatra One typography. Outlined with a high-contrast teak edge and flanked by geometric 'NX' bracket flourishes. Pressed states simulate mechanical pressure via inward 1px translation and full color saturation.
- **Secondary Action (Block Stamp):** Transparent ground enclosed in a 1.5px dabu indigo (`#2b3a67`) border, styled with subtle ink-bleed misregistration. Text rendered in bold uppercase Karla.
- **Tertiary Action (Thread Stitched):** Flat textile sand (`#e8d8b8`) with an open 1px dashed marigold border (`#f0a202`), resembling hand-sewn basting threads.

### Chips & Swatch Badges
- **Shot Status Tags:** Cut like bespoke fabric inventory tags. Off-white ground (`#fcfbf7`), 1px dashed teak border, fixed with a circular "rivet" or "stitch knot" graphic at the left edge.
- **Variants:** Approved (Dabu Indigo tint), Revision Required (Madder Red wash), In-Progress (Marigold fiber tint).

### Input Fields & Storyboard Annotation Rails
- **Script & Note Inputs:** Inset panels simulating smooth lime-plaster surfaces (`#fcfbf7`) against the rough cotton background. Framed by a solid 1px teak border. Placeholder text appears in a 40% faded indigo ink. Focused states illuminate the inner border with a crisp 1.5px marigold (`#f0a202`) line.

### Lists & Director Cue Sheets
- **Timeline Cues:** Stacked horizontal strips separated not by hairline rules, but by continuous micro-jaali geometric ribbon prints (repeating chevron or 'NX' diamond lattice).
- **Audio / FX Markers:** Represented by scalloped leaf markers stamped directly into the margin.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Selectors
- **Toggles:** Styled as small brass or teak block stamps. Unchecked states show an empty carved square outline; checked states display a solid, stamped geometric sun-medallion or 'NX' brandmark in Manjistha red.
