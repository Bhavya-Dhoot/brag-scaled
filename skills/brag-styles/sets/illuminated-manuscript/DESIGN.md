## Brand & Style

This design system translates the artisanal rigor of the late Tudor scriptorium into a living digital medium. The style is **tactile historicism fused with speculative secular scholarship**: authentic vellum surfaces, meticulous leadpoint ruling, hand-ground mineral pigments, and illuminated marginalia harmonize with modern interface paradigms. 

The emotional tone evokes reverent craft, quiet focus, and intellectual discovery. It targets scholars, archivists, digital humanists, and technologists who value depth, tactile heritage, and wit. Whimsical marginalia—drolleries and chimerae clutching silicon chips, styluses, and micro-circuitry—subvert archaic solemnity, declaring the interface an active workshop of future-facing secular knowledge marked by the continuous `NX` scribe's cipher.

## Colors

The palette reproduces authentic mineral and biological pigments prepared according to historical scriptorium recipes:

- **Primary (`#a8231d` - Rubric Vermilion):** Sourced from cinnabar. Reserved for focal hierarchy, active states, key interactive indicators, paragraphus marks, and rubricated headings.
- **Secondary (`#264a8c` - Lapis Ultramarine):** Deep crushed lapis lazuli. Denotes intellectual state transitions, auxiliary actions, selected items, and illuminated flourishes.
- **Tertiary (`#c79a2b` - Burnished Gold Leaf):** Leaf gilding applied over gesso grounds. Used for borders, ornamental fillets, badges, status highlights (`#fbe893`), and shaded incised shadows (`#8d6b14`).
- **Neutral (`#2a1f16` - Iron-Gall Ink):** Acidic tannic oak-gall ink oxidized to a resonant warm brownish-black. Dictates all core typography, borders, and penwork.
- **Surface Foundations:**
  - Base Vellum Ground: `#efe2c3`
  - Elevated Container Vellum: `#f7edd5`
  - Recessed / Inset Folio Dim: `#e4d4ae`
- **Scriptorium Accent (`#2d5a3f` - Malachite Green):** Copper carbonate pigment used strictly for system confirmations, safe states, and vegetal tendrils.
- **Leadpoint Ruling:** Translucent iron-tinted guidelines set at `#2a1f16` with 12% opacity.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy mirrors Tudor scholarly publishing:

- **Display & Section Openers:** Rendered in **EB Garamond** with small-caps styling, generous leading, and authentic italic ligatures. For isolated capital drop-caps and major foliation breaks, historic blackletter glyphs or illuminated initial squares frame the entry text.
- **Body & Continuous Text:** **EB Garamond** balances academic weight with optical clarity. Long-form copy must adhere strictly to horizontal baseline rhythms derived from leadpoint ruling.
- **Rubrics & Signifiers:** Set in all-caps or small-caps with generous tracking (`title-rubric`) in vermilion red (`#a8231d`) to mark sections, navigational breadcrumbs, and chapter arguments.
- **Technical Marginalia & Metadata:** **JetBrains Mono** (`label-scholastic`) renders system codes, indices, cryptographic hashes, and the `NX` scribe's marks, acting as an intentional bridge between Tudor scriveners and modern digital computation.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout geometry follows the classical proportions of the *Canons of Page Construction* (Van de Graaf canon). 

- **Grid Framework:** A symmetrical 12-column layout on desktop scales down to 6 columns on tablet and 2 columns on mobile. Margins mirror binding gutters: inner margins are compact, outer margins broad to host glosses, prickings, and marginalia.
- **Leadpoint Baseline System:** All structural vertical spacing snaps to an `8px` / `0.5rem` baseline grid, visually manifested through subtle leadpoint ruled lines across document surfaces.
- **Marginalia Column:** On desktop viewports (`>= 1280px`), an asymmetrical 4-column dedicated gloss gutter reserves space for drolleries, metadata, footnote annotations, and the `NX` scribe verification seal.
- **Prick Holes & Ruled Boundaries:** Outer container wrappers exhibit simulated prick marks evenly distributed along the extreme margin edge to anchor the scriptorium illusion.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids modern diffuse drop shadows, opting instead for medieval layered physical assemblies:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Desk Foundation (Canvas):** `#efe2c3` (vellum ground).
  - **Nested Foliation (Cards/Containers):** Raised parchment panels in `#f7edd5` framed by razor-thin `1px` borders in `#2a1f16` (opacity 0.25) or double-filleted gold leaf borders (`#c79a2b`).
  - **Recessed Areas (Inputs/Panels):** Inset scrapings in `#e4d4ae` with internal top-left shadows simulating scraped skin: `inset 1px 2px 4px rgba(42, 31, 22, 0.12)`.
- **Gilded Illumination & Burnishing:** Interactive elements and illuminated borders use dual specular reflections:
  - Base filleting: `0 1px 0 #fbe893, inset 0 1px 0 #8d6b14`.
  - Floating states (Modals/Overlays): Deep parchment stacking with low-blur, warm umber shadows: `0 4px 16px rgba(42, 31, 22, 0.18), 0 12px 32px rgba(42, 31, 22, 0.1)`.

## Shapes

Shapes adhere strictly to **sharp, hand-cut vellum edges (`roundedness: 0`)**. 

- Parchment sheets, cards, buttons, tabs, and input borders are rectangular and orthogonal, reflecting trimmed sheepskin leaves.
- Internal embellishments introduce curves organically through decorative SVG filigree, 45-degree chamfered corners on gilded badges, and circular wax seals for authentication stamps.
- Structural container dividers utilize hairline leadpoint scores or repeating vegetal vine flourishes rather than pill outlines.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Rubric Vermilion):** Solid `#a8231d` background, `#f7edd5` text, crisp `1px` border in `#8d6b14`. On hover, the background transitions to oxidized cinnabar `#8f1d18` with an inset burnished gold shimmer outline.
- **Secondary (Lapis Seal):** Deep `#264a8c` ground with `#f7edd5` typography, bordered with hairline gold leaf. Hover shifts toward indigo `#1e3b70`.
- **Tertiary (Scribe’s Quills):** Transparent background, `#2a1f16` ink text, framed by dashed leadpoint rulings that turn solid under hover, accompanied by a miniature pen-stroke icon.

### Form Inputs
- Rendered as vellum mortises: background `#e4d4ae`, inner shadow simulating recessed scraped skin, bottom border only in solid `#2a1f16` (`1.5px`).
- Focused state: Border turns Vermilion (`#a8231d`) with a glowing leadpoint guide rule extending across the bottom; floating labels take the `label-scholastic` style in gold-brown.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkboxes appear as hand-drawn ink squarings `[ ]`. Active state displays an authentic diagonal cross-stroke rubric `[X]` rendered in vermilion red.
- Radio buttons appear as double-ringed astrological compasses, filled with a solid lapis center dot when selected.

### Cards & Folio Enclosures
- Bound in `#f7edd5` with an outer border composed of a double-line leadpoint frame (`#2a1f16` at 20%) punctuated with gold leaf leaf-knot accents in each corner.
- Optional header ribbons with alternating red and blue pen flourishes.

### Chips & Badges
- Sharp-edged rectangular slips styled after archival parchment tags.
- Categorical chips use malachite green (`#2d5a3f`), lapis (`#264a8c`), or vermilion (`#a8231d`) borders with faint tinted vellum infill and `label-scholastic` uppercase text.

### Illuminated Drop-Caps
- Large 3-line structural initials rendered in gold leaf and ultramarine filigree containing intricate acanthus scrollwork, anchoring the start of significant content blocks.

### Marginalia & Scribe’s Cipher (`NX`)
- The secular `NX` cipher appears as an embossed authentication watermark or signature line on bottom margins.
- Secular drolleries (rabbits wielding solder irons, snails carrying microchips) sit anchored dynamically within the outer margin grid.
