# Ethereal Haze

Soft light and pale colour: words resolve out of a haze as a sweep of light passes.

- **Asked for as:** ethereal, dreamy, airy, celestial, soft glow, angelic
- **Best for:** wellness, beauty, skincare, meditation, weddings, ambient music. Weak for B2B
- **Palette:** pearl `#F6F3FA`, lavender `#D9D2F5`, blush `#F7D6E0`, sky `#CFE6F7`, soft gold `#E8D9A8`, text plum `#3B3550`
- **Type:** Instrument Serif with wide tracking for titles; Inter 400 for the rest
- **Texture:** a slow colour mesh, soft bloom, hairlines, dust motes rising
- **Signature move:** light. A soft sweep crosses the frame and the words resolve from blur as it passes; everything floats on a slow drift
- **Transitions:** bloom to white, then resolve
- **Sound:** pad and strings, no bed; `chime`
- **Watch out:** white text on pastel is unreadable: keep text plum. Slow styles audit as slideshows, so the mesh, the motes and a constant push never stop. Blur is costly: blur one element at a time
