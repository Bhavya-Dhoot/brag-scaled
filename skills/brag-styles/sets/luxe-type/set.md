# Luxe Type

Luxury typography: a very large serif on black, hairlines, and one word in champagne.

- **Asked for as:** luxury typography, luxury, high fashion, editorial luxury, elegant serif
- **Best for:** fashion, fragrance, jewellery, hotels, premium launches, invitations
- **Palette:** black `#0B0B0B`, ivory `#F3EEE4`, one metal: champagne `#C8B68A`. Nothing else
- **Type:** Instrument Serif at very large sizes, roman and italic mixed; widely tracked Inter capitals for small labels
- **Texture:** hairline rules, generous margins, a slow sheen crossing the champagne word
- **Signature move:** the reveal. Lines rise from behind a hairline one at a time, an italic word slides in to replace a roman one, and a sheen passes across the single champagne word
- **Transitions:** the screen inverts from black to ivory along a moving hairline
- **Sound:** strings and a single piano note, no bed; `chime`
- **Watch out:** one idea a screen and no more than seven words. Slow styles audit as slideshows: keep the sheen and a slow push running
