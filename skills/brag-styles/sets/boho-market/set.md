# Boho Market

Bohemian: woven bands, paper-cut arches, a sun and tassels, in warm earth colours.

- **Asked for as:** bohemian, boho, earthy, folk, festival, artisan
- **Best for:** travel, handmade goods, cafes, festivals, lifestyle, makers' marketplaces. Weak for B2B
- **Palette:** terracotta `#C8553D`, mustard `#E0A526`, sage `#8A9A5B`, indigo `#2B3A67`, cream `#F4EBDD`, plum `#6D2E46`
- **Type:** Instrument Serif italic for headlines, Bricolage Grotesque 800 for labels, each set a degree or two off level
- **Texture:** woven bands (stripes, zigzags and diamonds from repeating gradients), paper-cut arches and rainbows, a sun, tassels; edges slightly uneven
- **Signature move:** weaving. Patterned strips slide in from alternate sides and lock into a rug the words sit on; arches stack up like cut paper and tassels sway
- **Transitions:** a patterned band wipes across
- **Sound:** marimba and plucks over `boombap` at 92 BPM; `pop`, `tick`
- **Watch out:** build patterns from plain geometry; do not copy sacred or tribal motifs from a specific culture
