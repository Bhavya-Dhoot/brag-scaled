# Museum Placard

The product exhibited as fine art: white cube, a spotlight, a wall label. Pairs with the `deadpan` tone.

- **Best for:** design tools, premium products, anything that deserves to be taken seriously
- **Palette:** gallery white `#f5f4f0`, shadow `#d9d6ce`, label ink `#222222`, one spotlight warm `#fff4d6`
- **Type:** Inter Tight for placards (the gallery convention), Instrument Sans for titles
- **Texture:** soft floor-to-wall gradient, a spotlight cone, frame shadow
- **Signature move:** the camera glides between "artworks" (the product's screens) with long holds, and the placard fades in: title, year, medium ("TypeScript on Postgres") and a deadpan curator's note
- **Transitions:** a slow lateral gallery walk; nothing cuts
- **Sound:** room tone, distant footsteps, a single sustained piano note
- **Watch out:** it needs holds of 3 seconds or more. Rushing it kills the joke.
