# Extra! Extra!

The launch as breaking news: a broadsheet front page, a headline spinning in, halftone

photos, column rules.
- **Best for:** big announcements, B2B launches, data or insight products
- **Palette:** newsprint `#ece6d6`, ink `#161616`, spot red `#c1272d` for one word only
- **Type:** Playfair Display Black for the masthead and headlines (a serif is right here: newspaper heritage); Libre Franklin for body; Courier Prime for datelines
- **Texture:** column rules, halftone photos, fold crease, slight ink bleed
- **Signature move:** the classic spinning newspaper that stops dead on the headline, then the camera pushes into one column
- **Transitions:** a page turn, or a spin between front pages
- **Sound:** a newsroom teletype ticking, a press rumble, and a big band sting on the headline
- **Watch out:** body columns are texture, not something to read. Keep the real claims in the headline and deck.

## Reference kit (open only what you need)
- `frames/f1-f4.webp`: title, feature, stat and end card. Use them for composition and ornament.
- `DESIGN.md`: Stitch's full token set. Open it only when you need an exact colour role or type size.
- `ref/f1-f4.html`: Tailwind reference markup. Lift the ornament (SVG, borders, textures), not the page layout.

## Audit notes
- Stitch frames load: Courier Prime, Libre Franklin, Playfair Display. Use the **Type** line above; it is the intended pairing.
- 16:9 fit at 1920x1080: all four frames fit.
- Sanitised on import: 2x placeholder links must not point at a registrable domain.
- `assets/` holds 2 Stitch-generated image(s): mood reference only, never in the video.
- Every word in the Stitch frames is placeholder copy: NEXUS, nexus.example, 99.999%, 840 ns, SOC 2, "certified", "granted". Replace all of it with the project's own facts.
