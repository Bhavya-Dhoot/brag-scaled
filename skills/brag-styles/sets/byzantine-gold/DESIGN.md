## Brand & Style

This design system translates the monumental, secular imperial revetments of Ravenna and Constantinople into a digital visual language. The aesthetic draws strictly from imperial statecraft, architectural geometry, lapidary craftsmanship, and tectonic masonry—completely devoid of ecclesiastical iconography, halos, or religious figurative forms. 

The emotional response is one of solemn permanence, tactile luxury, and ceremonial precision. The aesthetic movement is **Tactile / Skeuomorphic Mosaic Structuralism**: surfaces mimic the irregular cant and hand-pressed tilt of gold-leaf glass tesserae embedded in deep hydraulic mortar. Interfaces do not float as weightless planes; they read as load-bearing slabs, opus sectile inlays, and micro-faceted surfaces reflecting warm, directional, specular light against deep lapis and porphyry reserves.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in raw minerals, smalti glass, and architectural ground:

- **Primary (`#d4a73a`) — Aurum Smalti**: The foundational gold leaf encased in glass. Accompanied by shaded ochre (`#9c7419`) for tessera facets in shadow and brilliant specular gold (`#f7d578`) for raking light edges, active states, and elevated focus indicators.
- **Secondary (`#1c2f6b`) — Lapis Lazuli**: Deep, saturated cobalt blue extracted from Afghan lapis. Used for authoritative badges, high-priority state actions, and secondary focal containers.
- **Tertiary (`#6e2033`) — Imperial Porphyry**: The dense purple-red Egyptian stone reserved strictly for imperial decrees, destructive or critical actions, and ceremonial emphasis.
- **Neutral (`#1a1510`) — Umber Grout & Smoked Basalt**: The base canvas and interstitial grout mortar. Deep charcoal warmed with raw umber undertones. Surfaces step up through `#241e17` (mortar bed) to `#2f271f` (stone container).
- **Text & Accent Neutral (`#ece8df`) — Antique Marble**: Fine-grained Proconnesian white marble used for high-contrast legible body text, delicate hairline rules, and crisp geometric glyphs.

Surfaces use multi-stop linear and radial gradients mimicking light hitting faceted, hand-placed tesserae rather than flat single-color fills.

## Typography

Typography establishes an architectural order. Headlines and titles employ classical Roman and Carolingian proportions via **EB Garamond**, styled with uppercase tracking, small capitals, and balanced weights to evoke monumental bronze inscriptions and imperial lapidary chancery.

Body text retains **EB Garamond** for scholarly, historical, and narrative cadence, ensuring high readability against dark mineral backdrops. 

Functional UI telemetry, metadata, tabular numbers, and micro-labels utilize **Space Grotesk** set in uppercase with wide tracking (`0.12em`–`0.16em`). This introduces sharp lapidary geometry that complements stone masonry without visual distraction.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an **opus sectile architectural grid**—a strict structural foundation where panels, revetments, and bands align along axial sightlines reminiscent of Byzantine court halls.

- **Grid Architecture**: A 12-column fluid grid on desktop (`1280px` max container) that reduces to 8 columns on tablet (`768px` to `1023px`) and 4 columns on mobile (`<768px`).
- **Grout Line Rhythms**: Component gaps and gutter intervals strictly match multiples of the tessera gap module (`0.25rem` / `4px`). Panels sit edge-to-edge separated by explicit `1px` or `2px` umber mortar seams (`#1a1510`).
- **Symmetry & Framing**: High-value displays employ bilateral symmetry, flanked by ornamental border tracks containing guilloche braids or repeating diamond tessellations. Outer canvases maintain a substantial perimeter margin (`3.5rem` on desktop) creating the presence of a framed wall alcove.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth does not rely on soft, atmospheric drop shadows common in modern SaaS. Instead, elevation mirrors **tectonic layering and raking light across three-dimensional mosaic relief**:

1. **Mortar Bed (Base Level - Canvas)**: Deep `#1a1510` textured ground. Recessed elements sit inset with a `1px` inner shadow (`inset 0 2px 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.85)`).
2. **Inlaid Revetment (Tier 1 - Cards & Panels)**: Grounded plates raised by `1px` solid chiseled borders. The upper and left edges capture light via `#f7d578` (opacity 0.25), while the bottom and right edges drop into umber shadow (`#0d0a08`).
3. **Mounted Opus Sectile (Tier 2 - Modals & Floating Shelves)**: Dual-step physical elevation using a high-density, sharp contact shadow (`0 4px 0 #0f0c08, 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(0,0,0,0.9)`) combined with a faint 1px outer rim of burnished gold.
4. **Specular Micro-Faceted Sheen**: Hover and focus states do not simply brighten; they project a directional glint (`linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(247,213,120,0.4) 0%, transparent 60%)`), simulating candlelight grazing unevenly set smalti cubes.

## Shapes

The geometry is strictly **chiseled stone and tessellated glass: 0px border-radius across all system components**. Rounded corners contradict the physics of cut hardstone and glass smalti.

- **Corners**: Razor 90-degree miters. For specialized focal containers and badges, corners employ 45-degree chamfers (`clip-path: polygon(...)`) sized at `6px` or `8px` to emulate cut lapis corner-plates.
- **Borders**: Thin, defined, architectural lines (`1px` to `2px`). Primary boundaries use dual-line framing: an outer deep umber line paired with an inner metallic gold hairline (`#d4a73a`, 20% opacity).
- **Guilloche & Interlace Accents**: Section dividers feature SVG geometric interlace bands, wave crests, or stepped key-meanders rather than generic horizontal rules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Imperial Primary (Gold Smalti)**: Background is an angled gradient (`#d4a73a` to `#9c7419`). Label is set in Space Grotesk (`label-lg`), colored `#1a1510` in heavy bold weight. Border is `1px` solid `#f7d578`. Top edge features an inner highlight line. Hover triggers an intense gold luster transition with a specular rim flare.
- **Cobalt Secondary (Lapis)**: Background is `#1c2f6b` bordered in `#d4a73a` (`1px`). Label is `#ece8df`. Hover yields an inner lapis glow and brightens the gold outline to `#f7d578`.
- **Porphyry Tertiary (Imperial Action)**: Deep `#6e2033` ground framed with a thin `#9c7419` border. Used for high-stakes operational commits or critical cancellations.
- **Sectile Ghost**: Transparent background, bordered by a dashed or solid antique marble hairline (`#ece8df` at 30%), text in `#ece8df`.

### Chips & Badges
- Sharp rectangular tiles with `1px` solid metallic rims.
- Backgrounds use dark mineral tones: Deep Lapis (`rgba(28, 47, 107, 0.4)`), Porphyry (`rgba(110, 32, 51, 0.4)`), or Raw Umber (`#241e17`).
- Text in Space Grotesk `label-sm` with letter spacing `0.16em`.

### Lists & Tables
- Table headers mimic architrave lintels: `#241e17` ground, bordered top and bottom with a 1px gold rail (`#9c7419`), typography in uppercase `label-md`.
- Rows alternate between `#1a1510` and `#1f1913`. Row hover casts a raking gold specular gradient (`rgba(212, 167, 58, 0.08)`) from the left edge.
- Cells separated by hairline umber grout seams (`#2b221a`).

### Form Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes**: Pure square `#1a1510` box, `1.5px` border in `#9c7419`. Checked state reveals a solid `#d4a73a` tessera stone center flanked by a sharp, square `#f7d578` inner core (no standard curved checkmarks; use a centered mini-square or crisp geometric diamond).
- **Radio Buttons**: Diamond orientation (square rotated 45 degrees, unrounded). Selected state illuminates an inner solid diamond in bright aurum `#f7d578`.

### Input Fields
- Chiseled stone trough design: background `#14100c` with an inset shadow (`inset 0 2px 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.9)`).
- Bottom border is active `1.5px` gold (`#9c7419`), side and top borders subdued umber (`#2b221a`).
- Text in Antique Marble `#ece8df`. Placeholder in muted ochre-tinted grey (`#877864`). Focus brings an animated hairline expansion of `#f7d578` with a subtle gold base reflection.

### Cards & Revetment Panels
- Base surface constructed of `#241e17` framed by a double perimeter: an exterior `1px` boundary of `#1a1510` and an interior `1px` gold filament (`#d4a73a` at 40%).
- Top header zones are capped by a stepped mosaic frieze or guilloche strip (`4px` height).
- Interactive cards reflect light upon pointer movement via dynamic CSS radial specular gradients.

### Specialized Architectural Components
- **Guilloche Frieze Divider**: A stylized SVG braided ribbon separating primary page views, rendered in duo-tone `#d4a73a` and `#1c2f6b`.
- **Tessera Progress Rail**: Linear loading states render not as smooth continuous bars, but as an advancing sequence of discrete, individually lit golden square smalti blocks.
