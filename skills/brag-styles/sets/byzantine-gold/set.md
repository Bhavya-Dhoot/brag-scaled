# Byzantine Gold

The product assembled tile by tile in a gold-ground mosaic.

- **Best for:** community and social products, "built piece by piece" stories, data made of many parts
- **Palette:** gold tesserae `#d4a73a` to `#9c7419`, deep blue `#1c2f6b`, porphyry `#6e2033`, white marble `#ece8df`
- **Type:** Cinzel Decorative for titles, Cormorant for body
- **Texture:** irregular tesserae with dark grout, gold tiles set at slight angles so they catch light differently
- **Signature move:** tesserae flip in tile by tile (canvas), each gold tile catching a travelling light sweep, and the image resolves from scattered tiles into the product
- **Transitions:** a tile-flip wave across the frame
- **Sound:** a choir pad (open fifths), deep bell, a glassy chime per tile burst, kept soft
- **Watch out:** it needs one canvas layer and a fixed tile grid, or it gets too heavy to render. No religious iconography.

## Audit notes
- Stitch frames load: EB Garamond, Space Grotesk. Use the **Type** line above; it is the intended pairing.
- 16:9 fit at 1920x1080: f1 is 1920x1645; f2 is 1920x1598; f3 is 1920x1654; f4 is 1920x1671. Recompose these for 16:9; never scale them down to fit.
- `assets/` holds 1 Stitch-generated image(s): mood reference only, never in the video.
- Every word in the Stitch frames is placeholder copy: NEXUS, nexus.example, 99.999%, 840 ns, SOC 2, "certified", "granted". Replace all of it with the project's own facts.
