# The Sultan's Firman

An imperial decree on marbled paper, crowned with a sweeping calligraphic monogram built from the product's own name.

- **Best for:** bold announcements, fintech and legal tech ("decreed"), anything that should feel official
- **Palette:** Iznik cobalt `#1f4e9c`, turquoise `#2aa3a8`, tomato red `#c73e1d`, gold `#c9a227`, ivory `#f4ecda`
- **Type:** Cinzel for Latin headings; an original swash monogram of the product name drawn as a stroke path
- **Texture:** *ebru* (marbled) paper borders, Iznik tile tulips and carnations, gilt rules
- **Signature move:** *ebru* marbling is generated live: drops spread, a comb drags them into feathers, and the paper lifts off. Then the monogram draws itself in one long gold stroke.
- **Transitions:** a new marbled sheet is lifted from the tray
- **Sound:** ney flute and oud in maqam Hijaz, *darbuka* on the beats, a low drum on the decree
- **Watch out:** do not imitate a real tughra or use Arabic script as decoration. The monogram is Latin and invented.
