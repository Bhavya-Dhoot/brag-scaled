## Brand & Style

This design system translates the monumental fusion of Art Deco architecture and Indian princely opulence found along Bombay’s 1930s Marine Drive, Oval Maidan cinema palaces (Eros, Regal), and royal sea-facing suites into a widescreen 16:9 cinematic storyboard environment. 

The aesthetic marries **High-Contrast Monumental Deco** with **Skeuomorphic Jewel-Craft**:
- **Atmosphere**: Midnight sea-air, deep black lacquer, radiant brass fretwork, and fluted glass illuminated by warm tungsten marquee lamps.
- **Tone**: Regal, theatrical, architectural, and unapologetically lavish yet structurally disciplined by strict geometry.
- **Graphic Devices**: Stepped ziggurats, sunburst radiator fans, nautical portholes, brass dial registers, and multi-line hairline rules.
- **Synthetic Regalia**: All insignia, escutcheons, and warrants are strictly invented. The signature crest is the geometric "NX" monogram forged from concentric chevron lines, stepped plinths, and radiant brass rays. Real royal coats of arms or historical state seals are strictly prohibited.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the opulent material palette of 1930s Indo-Deco interiors:

- **Black Lacquer (`#0d0d0d`)**: Primary canvas ground. Deep, light-absorbing obsidian lacquer that allows brass and jewel tones to project forward.
- **Gilt Brass & Gold Leaf (`#d1a74a`)**: Primary brand tone. Supported by highlighted polished brass (`#fce396`) on top bevels and deep cast antique brass shadows (`#8c6a1e`) on bottom contours.
- **Imperial Emerald (`#0f5c4d`)**: Secondary princely jewel tone. Evokes velvet tapestries, malachite surfaces, and glazed ceramic tiles of cinema lobbies.
- **Flamingo Pink Accent (`#e8837a`)**: Tertiary accent. Drawn from tropical sea sunsets, neon theater marquees, and dyed ostrich plumes; used strictly for focal callouts, active indicators, and high-glamour badges.
- **Warm Ivory Placard (`#f1e7d0`)**: Surface neutral. Represents antique vellum, bone inlay, and cream terrazzo placards, providing maximum legibility for dense descriptive text.

### Color Combinations & Rules
- Brass hairline borders (`#d1a74a`) must always encase Emerald (`#0f5c4d`) panels when mounted on Black Lacquer (`#0d0d0d`).
- Flamingo Pink (`#e8837a`) is strictly an accent (maximum 5% visual weight per 16:9 storyboard frame).
- Text on Black Lacquer defaults to Warm Ivory (`#f1e7d0`) or Polished Gold (`#fce396`).

## Typography

Typography balances monumental 1930s architectural capitals with clean, circular geometric body text:

- **Display & Headlines (`Poiret One`)**: Thin, elegant, geometric strokes reminiscent of etched brass elevator floor indicators and theater pediments. Always set in uppercase with tracked spacing (`0.08em` to `0.15em`).
- **Body (`Outfit`)**: Clean, low-contrast geometric sans-serif echoing early 20th-century modernist types (Century Gothic, Futura, Josefin Sans). Delivers effortless legibility against dark lacquer grounds.
- **Labels & Monograms (`Space Grotesk`)**: Technical, mechanical, and proportioned for dial readouts, frame stamps, catalog numbers, and stepped cartouches. Always uppercase with extended letter tracking (`0.18em` to `0.22em`).

## Layout & Spacing

This design system is tailored specifically for **16:9 widescreen presentation frames (1920x1080 fixed aspect ratio)**:

- **Canvas Structure**: The 1920x1080 canvas employs an outer frame margin of `4rem` (64px) demarcated by concentric stepped brass border rules.
- **Grid System**: A symmetrical 12-column fixed grid with `2rem` (32px) gutters, anchored around central architectural keystones and panoramic horizontal bands.
- **Ziggurat Rhythms**: Spacing between card groups and storyboard frame acts follows a stepped proportion: `0.5rem` (inner trim), `1rem` (panel padding), `1.5rem` (component gaps), `2.5rem` (module offsets), and `4rem` (scene section bounds).
- **Presentation Storyboard Safe Zone**: Critical narrative typography and interactive dials must reside within a 1792x952 inset box to guarantee framing on cinematic projection screens and displays.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use soft digital dropshadows. Instead, elevation mirrors physical architectural fabrication: marquetry inlay, stepped relief mouldings, etched glass, and brass metalwork.

- **Level 0 (Floor/Basework)**: Polished black lacquer `#0d0d0d`, treated with subtle dark terrazzo grain or vertical reeded texture lines.
- **Level 1 (Inlaid Panels & Terrazzo)**: Sunken or flush emerald green `#0f5c4d` panels, bordered by 1px solid brass `#d1a74a` inner rules.
- **Level 2 (Plaques & Storyboards)**: Warm Ivory `#f1e7d0` cards with a hard-edge 2px offset cast shadow in deep bronze-amber: `4px 4px 0px 0px rgba(140, 106, 30, 0.45)`.
- **Level 3 (Brass Grilles & Hardware)**: Dial indicators, porthole bezels, and the synthetic "NX" monogram float above the surface using a dual metallic edge-light: an upper highlight inset of `1px 1px 0px 0px #fce396` and a lower relief rim of `2px 2px 0px 0px #8c6a1e`.
- **Fluted Glass Overlays**: Floating inspection lenses and modal plaques employ a linear 4px reed gradient overlay backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a 1px border of `#fce396`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary is strictly architectural and geometric:

- **Base Radius**: `0px` (Sharp). Structural elements avoid rounded corners.
- **Stepped Ziggurat Corners**: Primary cards and placard corners use chamfered 45-degree cuts or notched stepped corners (8px x 8px stepped insets) emulating Mumbai's cinema rooflines.
- **Nautical & Aerodynamic Circularity**: Pure geometry is permitted only in absolute forms: circular portholes (`aspect-ratio: 1/1; border-radius: 9999px`), semicircular sunburst fan headers, and dial rings.
- **Linear Motifs**: Double and triple concentric hairline borders (1px rule, 3px gap, 1px rule) are the signature containment shape for all structural blocks.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Triggers
- **Primary (Gilt Stepped Plinth)**: Deep emerald or black lacquer core, framed in a double 1px gold leaf rule `#d1a74a` with 45-degree chamfered corners. Label in `label-caps` gold `#fce396`. Hover triggers an inner brass glow and transitions the background to emerald `#0f5c4d`.
- **Secondary (Brass Hairline)**: Transparent background, 1px perimeter border in `#d1a74a`, Warm Ivory text `#f1e7d0`.
- **Glamour Action (Flamingo Focal)**: Solid `#e8837a` background with black lacquer text `#0d0d0d` in bold geometric capitals. Used for primary scene progression and act climaxes.

### 2. The "NX" Synthetic Crest & Monogram
- Rendered in multi-line chevron geometry.
- Centered atop storyboard scenes inside an arched brass crest or porthole bezel.
- Flanked by stylized geometric palm fronds or radiator sunburst lines; no historical heraldic beasts or royal crests are permitted.

### 3. Storyboard Placards & Cards
- **Ivory Placard Card**: Warm Ivory `#f1e7d0` surface, 1px inner gold border, typography in `#0d0d0d` lacquer. Features top and bottom brass corner bracket hardware graphics.
- **Cinema Suite Frame Card**: Deep black lacquer `#0d0d0d` card bordered with stepped ziggurat chevron framing in `#d1a74a`, containing 16:9 visual concept stills with an etched glass descriptor plate below.

### 4. Elevator Dial Floor Indicators & Scrims
- Semicircular brass arc registers mounted at the head of scenes to indicate storyboard sequence and act progression (e.g., "ACT I — THE HARBOUR PROMENADE").
- Mechanical brass pointer needle tipped with a Flamingo Pink gem marker `#e8837a`.

### 5. Input Fields & Selectors
- Recessed rectangular slots in `#0d0d0d` lacquer with an inset bottom-edge brass bar (`#d1a74a`).
- Placeholder text set in italicized `Outfit` muted gold (`#8c6a1e`). Focus shifts bottom bar to brilliant highlight `#fce396` with a faint emerald backlight.

### 6. Chips & Status Badges
- Hexagonal or notched rectangular badges.
- Background in translucent Emerald (`#0f5c4d` at 30% opacity), enclosed by a 1px brass frame with `label-sm` text tracking at `0.22em`. Active status marked with an `#e8837a` flamingo dot.
