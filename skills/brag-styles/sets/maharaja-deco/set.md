# Maharaja Deco

Art Deco as the princely states built it: Marine Drive facades, sunbursts, black lacquer and gold.

- **Best for:** premium launches, hospitality and finance, anything glamorous with rigour
- **Palette:** black lacquer `#0d0d0d`, gold `#d1a74a`, emerald `#0f5c4d`, ivory `#f1e7d0`, flamingo accent `#e8837a`
- **Type:** Limelight or Poiret One for titles, Josefin Sans for body
- **Texture:** sunburst fans, stepped ziggurat frames, terrazzo floor, porthole windows, etched glass
- **Signature move:** a lift-door reveal. The Deco brass doors slide apart, the floor-indicator needle swings to the next feature's "floor", and sunburst rays fan out behind the claim.
- **Transitions:** the lift doors close and open; the floor number counts
- **Sound:** a 1930s swing band with muted trumpet and brushed drums, a lift bell "ding" in key on each floor, clarinet on the CTA
- **Watch out:** gold on black needs a heavy weight and large sizes. Thin gold type disappears.

## Reference kit (open only what you need)
- `frames/f1-f4.webp`: title, feature, stat and end card. Use them for composition and ornament.
- `DESIGN.md`: Stitch's full token set. Open it only when you need an exact colour role or type size.
- `ref/f1-f4.html`: Tailwind reference markup. Lift the ornament (SVG, borders, textures), not the page layout.
- `frames/maharaja_deco_monogram_crest.webp`: a standalone emblem to rebuild as SVG with the project's own initials.

## Audit notes
- Stitch frames load: Outfit, Poiret One, Space Grotesk. Use the **Type** line above; it is the intended pairing.
- 16:9 fit at 1920x1080: f1 is 1920x1285; f2 is 1920x1238; f3 is 1920x1104; f4 is 1920x1376. Recompose these for 16:9; never scale them down to fit.
- Sanitised on import: 1x placeholder links must not point at a registrable domain.
- `assets/` holds 2 Stitch-generated image(s): mood reference only, never in the video.
- Every word in the Stitch frames is placeholder copy: NEXUS, nexus.example, 99.999%, 840 ns, SOC 2, "certified", "granted". Replace all of it with the project's own facts.
