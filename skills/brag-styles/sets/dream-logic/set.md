# Dream Logic

Surrealism: a low horizon, long shadows, objects at impossible scale, and a door that opens onto the next scene.

- **Asked for as:** surrealism, surreal, dreamlike, Magritte-like, uncanny
- **Best for:** art, fashion, fragrance, creative studios, concept announcements, products about imagination. It needs a metaphor that explains the product in one line
- **Palette:** dusk sky from `#F2C6A0` to `#7FA6C9`, sand `#E8D3B0`, deep teal `#12343B`, vermilion `#E4572E`, bone `#F7F1E5`
- **Type:** Instrument Serif italic, large; Inter capitals, small and tracked, for labels
- **Texture:** a low horizon, long shadows, a checkerboard floor in perspective, a door standing alone, a moon too large, stairs that stop
- **Signature move:** the door. Scale and gravity break (the object floats, shadows swing as the light moves), then a door opens and the camera goes through it into the next scene
- **Transitions:** always through an opening: a door, a window, a picture frame
- **Sound:** a slow pad, no beat; `chime`, and `whoosh` through the door
- **Watch out:** no melting clocks and no copied paintings. If the image does not explain the product, it is only strange
