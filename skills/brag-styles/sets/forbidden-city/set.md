# Forbidden City

Imperial yellow, vermilion lacquer, a scroll that unrolls horizontally and closes with a red seal chop.

- **Best for:** launches aimed at China or Asia, craft and precision products, calm and confident brands
- **Palette:** imperial yellow `#f2b705`, vermilion lacquer `#b3261e`, jade `#3c8d6e`, ink `#1a1a1a`, silk `#f3ead3`
- **Type:** Noto Serif SC for any Chinese; Cormorant SC for Latin titles; Ma Shan Zheng for brush accents
- **Texture:** silk weave, rolled scroll ends with jade knobs, cloud (*xiangyun*) motifs, lattice windows
- **Signature move:** a horizontal handscroll unrolls and the camera pans across it scene by scene; each claim is sealed with a red chop that stamps and bleeds in
- **Transitions:** the continuous pan is the transition; the scroll rolls up at the end
- **Sound:** guzheng in the pentatonic scale, a soft gong on scene changes, the woodblock of the seal stamp
- **Watch out:** only use Chinese text that is real and correct (checked by a speaker) or none at all. Never use fake characters.

## Reference kit (open only what you need)
- `frames/f1-f4.webp`: title, feature, stat and end card. Use them for composition and ornament.
- `DESIGN.md`: Stitch's full token set. Open it only when you need an exact colour role or type size.
- `ref/f1-f4.html`: Tailwind reference markup. Lift the ornament (SVG, borders, textures), not the page layout.
- `frames/imperial_cinnabar_seal_chop_nx.webp`: a standalone emblem to rebuild as SVG with the project's own initials.

## Audit notes
- Stitch frames load: EB Garamond, Manrope, Noto Serif. Use the **Type** line above; it is the intended pairing.
- 16:9 fit at 1920x1080: f1 is 1920x1152; f3 is 1920x1144; f4 is 1920x1190. Recompose these for 16:9; never scale them down to fit.
- Sanitised on import: 1x no unverified Chinese characters; 2x no unverified Chinese characters.
- `assets/` holds 3 Stitch-generated image(s): mood reference only, never in the video.
- Every word in the Stitch frames is placeholder copy: NEXUS, nexus.example, 99.999%, 840 ns, SOC 2, "certified", "granted". Replace all of it with the project's own facts.
