## Brand & Style

This design system translates the ceremonial dignity, architectural geometry, and calligraphic restraint of the imperial court into a contemporary 16:9 widescreen presentation canvas. Designed specifically for narrative pacing and product unveilings, it evokes a tranquil majesty—patient, deliberate, and authoritative. 

The aesthetic merges **Tactile Classical Materiality** with **Architectural Minimalism**. Structural layouts evoke Chinese imperial wooden lattice architecture (*huntian jing*) and expansive horizontal handscrolls (*shoujuan*). Interfaces reject fast-paced app idioms in favor of intentional cinematic staging, sweeping continuous unrolling narratives, and generous negative space (*liubai*). 

The emotional resonance is quiet reverence: every element feels crafted from raw silk, hand-carved jade, cinnabar lacquer, and concentrated ink stone. Visual metaphors must strictly adhere to authentic material craftsmanship—ruling out kitsch, dragons, and faux-calligraphic glyphs in favor of authentic lattice ratios, genuine stamp iconography, and balanced typographies.

## Colors

The palette directly references the court materials of the Palace Museum:

- **Primary (`#B3261E` - Vermilion Lacquer):** Used for focal emphasis, cinnabar signature seals (*yinzhang*), major interactive commitments, and narrative chapter markers.
- **Secondary (`#F2B705` - Imperial Yellow & Gold Leaf):** Reserved for framing rules, gilded plaque lettering, metallic hardware accents, and key telemetry highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#3C8D6E` - Hetian Jade):** Applied to secondary indicators, status badges, cylindrical roller hardware details, and measured interactive states.
- **Neutral Ground (`#F3EAD3` - Aged Silk Fabric):** The universal background plane, evoking untreated raw ceremonial silk and mulberry paper.
- **Deep Calligraphy Ink (`#1A1A1A`):** The primary value for title glyphs, structural linework, and high-priority copy.
- **Ink Wash Middle (`#4A4A4A`):** Secondary specs, metadata, and structural lattice frames.
- **Ink Wash Pale (`#8C857B`):** Ghost rules, watermark grids, disabled states, and delicate cloud wash (*xiangyun*) motifs.

Backgrounds feature subtle paper tooth and silk-grain textural overlays. Avoid pure chromatic digital whites or flat blacks. Contrast must satisfy readability while preserving the feeling of dry ink pressed into fibrous silk.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy coordinates three distinct disciplines:
1. **Classical Latin Display Titles:** Handled by Noto Serif with small-cap styling and expanded letter-spacing to match the dignity of imperial stone carvings.
2. **Expository Body Prose:** Handled by EB Garamond, conveying literary humanism, generous line height, and contemplative pacing.
3. **Telemetry & Technical Annotations:** Set in Manrope, providing crisp, clean geometric contrast for dimensional specs, stage numbering, and navigation indicators.

All display titles and section banners are set with deliberate tracking (`0.06em` to `0.14em`), preventing visual crowding. Vertical typesetting modes can be utilized for side chapter markers and stamp attributions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is architected around a strict **16:9 widescreen canvas** modeled after imperial handscroll orientation. Content progresses horizontally along an expansive continuous coordinate system rather than through standard vertical web stacking.

- **Grid Architecture:** A 16-column continuous fluid layout framed within a fixed aspect ratio container. Column gutters scale at `2rem`, flanked by minimum lateral margins of `4rem` (`space-xl`), creating dedicated zones for handscroll rollers, imperial borders, and seal stamps.
- **Rhythm & Negative Space (*Liubai*):** Layouts must leave at least 40% of the active frame unoccupied. Information clusters gather within architectural grid quadrants reminiscent of palace partition screens (*gechan*).
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Desktop / Presentation (16:9 fixed ratio):** Full horizontal continuity with lateral panning controls and pinned structural roller headers.
  - **Tablet:** 8-column layout maintaining horizontal card swiping with compressed `2.5rem` margins.
  - **Mobile:** Reflows into a continuous scroll with sticky chapter headers, `1.5rem` outer margins, and horizontal sub-galleries for technical specifications.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through physical materials and layered planar overlaps rather than ambient digital drop-shadows:

- **Architectural Framing & Lattice Layers:** Depth is generated through concentric wood-lattice borders (*huntian jing*). Thin `1px` lines in Ink Wash Pale (`#8C857B`) and Ink Wash Middle (`#4A4A4A`) frame content panels, creating the illusion of peering through imperial palace screen windows.
- **Gilded Borders & Plaque Insets:** Elevated components (such as hero modal plaques) utilize a dual-border system: a `1px` outer rule in Vermilion Lacquer (`#B3261E`) paired with an inner `0.5px` inset border in Imperial Gold (`#F2B705`).
- **Material Shadows:** Where depth separation is required for floating cards or cinnabar stamps, use subtle, crisp directional underlays instead of blurred blurs: `0 2px 4px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.08)`, mimicking paper sheets laid flat upon silk.
- **Atmospheric Ink Wash Overlays:** Background environments evoke distance via low-opacity (5%–12%) layered SVG vector illustrations of curling clouds (*xiangyun*) and undulating mountain contours rendered in dry wash tones.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Sharp (Level 0)** standard, grounded in classical architectural carpentry, carved stone tablets (*bei*), and imperial horizontal signboards (*bian'e*).

- **Corners:** Rigorously 90-degree square corners across all cards, containers, input regions, and modal containers. 
- **Cinnabar Chop Seals (*Yinzhang*):** Geometric square shapes featuring custom stylized Latin monograms (`NX`) executed in authentic seal-script (*zhuanshu*) line geometry with inverted vermilion fills.
- **Roller Knobs & Accents:** Subtle cylindrical or notched forms occur strictly within ornamental hardware elements (such as the ends of handscroll mounting bars rendered in jade `#3C8D6E`), framed with gold fittings.
- **Lattice Cutouts:** Secondary modules may feature chamfered corner cuts (45-degree corner notches of `8px`) referencing Chinese window fretwork.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Imperial Button:** Rectangular vermilion lacquer fill (`#B3261E`) bordered by a `1px` gold inner line (`#F2B705`). Typography is set in uppercase `label-lg` in Aged Silk (`#F3EAD3`). Hover states deepen the lacquer to `#961F18` with subtle gold brightening.
- **Ghost Action:** Transparent background with deep calligraphy ink text (`#1A1A1A`), framed by a `1px` solid ink border (`#4A4A4A`). Hover transitions to a muted jade wash (`rgba(60, 141, 110, 0.1)`).

### Lacquer Plaques (*Bian'e*) & Cards
- Content containers are stylized as horizontal palace plaques. Flat Aged Silk ground (`#F3EAD3`) framed by double thin-line rules: outer vermilion or ink, inner gold leaf. Header bars feature centered titles flanked by symmetric geometric lattice vectors.

### Cinnabar Seals (*NX Yinzhang*)
- Interactive badges, approval chips, and verification watermarks are formatted as square red chops. Filled with Vermilion Lacquer (`#B3261E`), housing carved-out silk-colored lines that form the stylized `NX` monogram. These serve as narrative anchors, stage sign-offs, and authenticators for spec sheets.

### Chips & Metadata Tags
- Compact sharp tags framed in `1px` Jade (`#3C8D6E`) or Ink Wash (`#8C857B`). Text is set in `label-sm` Manrope uppercase. Chips indicate technical readiness, dimensions, materials, or sequence indices.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Square, border-bottom dominant or four-sided fine hairline frames (`1px` `#8C857B`). Backgrounds remain transparent over the silk ground. Focused fields transition their active border to vermilion lacquer with an adjacent miniature cinnabar indicator mark.

### Handscroll Navigation & Progress Bar
- The storyboard progression bar spans the bottom edge as a horizontal scroll axle. Jade roller knobs (`#3C8D6E`) capped with gold hardware anchor the extremes. The active scrubber reflects an unrolling silk surface marked with chapter intervals labeled in classical roman numerals and seal stamps.
