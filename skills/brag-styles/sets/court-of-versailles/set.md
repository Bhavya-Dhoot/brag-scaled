# Court of Versailles

Mirrors, gilt rocaille frames, velvet drapes: the launch as a royal reception in a hall of mirrors.

- **Best for:** luxury, premium SaaS with a sense of humour, "launch-day gala" moments
- **Palette:** ivory `#f5efe3`, gilt `#b8912f` (as a foil effect), rose `#d9a5a0`, royal blue velvet `#233a7a`, mirror silver `#cfd6dc`
- **Type:** Bodoni Moda for titles, Pinyon Script for one flourish per scene
- **Texture:** rocaille scrollwork frames, marble, candle glow and bloom, mirror reflections
- **Signature move:** velvet drapes part to reveal each claim inside an ornate gilt frame, the reflection repeats in a mirror behind it, and gilt catches a moving light
- **Transitions:** drapes close and open, or the camera glides down the Hall of Mirrors
- **Sound:** a harpsichord minuet in 3/4 with a string pad; fanfare trumpets on the reveal
- **Watch out:** gilt looks like mustard without a moving specular highlight. Animate the sheen.
