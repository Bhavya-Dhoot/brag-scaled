## Brand & Style

This design system channels the monumental splendor, ritual formality, and intricate craftsmanship of late 17th to mid-18th century French Baroque and Rococo court life, calibrated for modern digital experiences. It serves luxury curators, historical archives, high-end cultural institutions, and heritage houses demanding an atmosphere of uncompromising grandeur, sovereign ceremony, and theatrical intimacy.

The aesthetic philosophy fuses architectural precision with decorative excess:
- **Architectural Symmetry & Monumentality:** Layouts evoke the sweeping enfilades, fluted marble pilasters, and soaring archways of the Hall of Mirrors.
- **Rocaille Ornamentation:** Curves are motivated, sculpted, and dynamic—featuring stylized acanthus leaves, shell crests, and gilded framing fillets that gracefully encase content.
- **Invented Sovereign Regalia:** Symbolic ornamentation is strictly bespoke—relying on interlocking monograms (e.g., royal NX ciphers), solar rosettes, radiant burst motifs, and stylized classical fleurons. Historical real-world armorial bearings or heraldry are strictly forbidden.
- **Luminosity & Material Contrast:** Surfaces balance heavy royal sapphire velvet with calm, polished ivory marble panels to maintain supreme modern legibility against ornate gilded framing.

## Colors

The color palette translates the materials of the Sun King’s court into luminous digital surfaces:

- **Gilt Metallic Leaf (`primary_color_hex`: `#b8912f`):** Used for structural borders, ornamental crests, elevated interactive states, and royal ciphers. Supported by **Lustrous Warm Gold (`#ecd68a`)** for specular highlights and **Burnished Bronze (`#856118`)** for engraved shadows and metallic depth.
- **Royal Blue Velvet (`secondary_color_hex`: `#233a7a`):** Conveys sovereignty, depth, and prestige. Supported by **Deep Sapphire (`#132147`)** for rich evening canvas backdrops, contrast headers, and primary solid call-to-actions.
- **Dusty Rose (`tertiary_color_hex`: `#d9a5a0`):** Soft, powdered Rococo accent reminiscent of Madame de Pompadour's boudoir silk. Used for delicate status tags, secondary interactive highlights, and gentle decorative accents.
- **Ivory Marble (`neutral_color_hex`: `#f5efe3`):** The master canvas tone. High-clarity reading panels mimic polished Carrara and French limestone, offering a warm, paper-soft alternative to clinical stark white.
- **Mirror Silver (`#cfd6dc`):** Accents specular reflections, crisp inner fillet lines, and mercury-glass border treatments.
- **Court Ink (`#1e1a14`):** The primary typographic pigment—an extremely deep, warm charcoal that ensures crisp legibility without the jarring synthetic harshness of pure black.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy orchestrates a deliberate conversation between dynastic authority, courtly romance, and crisp utilitarian governance:

- **Bodoni Moda:** Assigned to all display titles, primary section headers, and imperial numbers. Its extreme stroke contrast, hairline serifs, and upright posture evoke monumental stone chiseling and early modern royal press editions.
- **EB Garamond:** Deployed across all continuous narrative, long-form reading, and descriptive cards. Its humanist, bookish cadence guarantees fatigue-free legibility against ivory surfaces. Italics should be used for curatorial epigraphs and subtle attributions.
- **Inter:** Reserved strictly for compact metadata, data grids, tabular financial figures, form labels, and system status tags. Styled with generous tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) and small-caps or uppercase transformations to prevent visual conflict with the neoclassical serifs.
- **Courtly Flourish Rule:** Display titles may occasionally be framed with an ornamental cursive drop-cap or single-line handwritten flourish. Reserve this strictly for one focal element per screen to preserve sovereign restraint and prevent decorative clutter.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an axial enfilade system—the classic French palace discipline of aligning doors, windows, and vistas along a grand central perspective line.

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views utilize a 12-column layout governed by formal symmetry. Primary content fields sit centered or flanked by dual pilaster rails. Mobile layouts compress to 4 columns while retaining generous outer margins to simulate framed canvas portraiture.
- **Enfilade Spacing Model:** White space is deliberate, monumental, and generous. Sections are demarcated not merely by borders, but by rhythmic vertical breathing room (`space-xl` and above).
- **Framed Canvases:** Rather than edge-to-edge fluid components, interface blocks sit recessed inside calm, elevated panels bordered by ornate margins, recreating the framed tapestry and boiserie paneling of Versailles chambers.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system is tactile, architectural, and illuminated by warm ambient candlelight rather than harsh digital drop shadows:

- **Candlelight Bloom:** Shadows feature an amber-gold undertone rather than neutral gray. Elevated containers project a soft, diffuse glow (`0 12px 32px -4px rgba(133, 97, 24, 0.12), 0 4px 12px rgba(35, 58, 122, 0.04)`), simulating warm beeswax candlelight reflecting off gilded molding.
- **Tonal Marble Tiering:** Depth stacks vertically through material:
  - *Base Canvas:* Rich Sapphire (`#132147`) or deep patterned damask.
  - *Primary Panels:* Honed Ivory Marble (`#f5efe3`) with subtle 1px inner gilt fillets.
  - *Secondary Floating Insets:* Frosted Mirror Glass with subtle silver-mercury translucency and high blur.
- **Gilded Fillet Outlines:** Elevation is reinforced with dual concentric borders: a primary 1px solid antique gold outer line (`#b8912f`) paired with an inner hairline margin in silver mirror (`#cfd6dc`) or pale gold highlight (`#ecd68a`), mimicking hand-carved wood boiserie.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary blends strict classical geometry with subtle Rococo curvature:

- **Soft Architectural Contours:** Components default to soft corners (`roundedness: 1`), keeping cards, dialogs, and panels structurally grounded like dressed stone masonry rather than plastic or rounded bubbles.
- **The Imperial Arch:** Prominent modal dialogs, featured media containers, and hero portrait cards employ full arch headpieces (`border-top-left-radius` and `border-top-right-radius` styled as semicircular vaults), recalling the arched mirrors and tall French casements of the Galerie des Glaces.
- **Scalloped Corners & Escutcheons:** Interactive action cards and luxury badges utilize custom clipped or indented corners (corner-notched geometry) capped with miniature Rocaille leaf vector terminals.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Sovereign Primary Button:** Deep Sapphire (`#233a7a`) background framed with a 1px Gilt Leaf (`#b8912f`) border, set with Bodoni Moda or high-tracked Inter text in Lustrous Gold (`#ecd68a`). On hover, the button displays a radiant candlelight bloom and subtle gold gradient shift.
- **Gilded Secondary Button:** Transparent ivory background encased in an ornate double-fillet gold outline with burnished corner flourishes. Text in Court Ink (`#1e1a14`).
- **Wax Seal / Monogram Action:** Circular or oval trigger bearing an embossed sovereign NX rosette, reserved for signature actions such as final approvals, royal charters, or document seals.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Marble Plinth Inputs:** Form fields reside within recessed Ivory Marble containers with a subtle inset shadow mimicking chiseled stone. Borders use muted Antique Bronze (`#856118`) that transitions to bright Gilded Leaf on focus, accompanied by a faint candlelight aura.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Checkboxes appear as diamond-cut escutcheons with an ornate gold tick. Radio buttons are styled as miniature sunburst rosettes with a glowing gold jewel core when selected.

### Cards & Boiserie Panels
- **Boiserie Panel Cards:** Content cards are enclosed in a layered ivory field with a raised gold-leaf border. Optional header treatments include an arched crest holding a single bespoke NX monogram or stylized shell flourish.
- **Velvet Drawer / Modal:** Heavy overlay sheets surfaced in Midnight Sapphire with dusty-rose cord dividers and gold tassel anchor graphics along the perimeter.

### Chips & Metadata Embellishments
- **Cartouche Chips:** Status tags resemble museum inventory cartouches—slender pill-shaped or notched ivory plates framed with hairline gold wire and set with Inter small-caps typography.
- **Fleuron Dividers:** Section separators discard generic gray rules in favor of a centered gold fleuron, solar flare, or stylized acanthus leaf bracket.
