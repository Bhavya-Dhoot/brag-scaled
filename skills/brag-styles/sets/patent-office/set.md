# Patent Office

The product filed as an invention: cyan blueprint, dimension lines, "FIG. 1" labels.

- **Best for:** hardware, APIs, developer tools, anything architectural
- **Palette:** blueprint `#0f3b73`, line white `#e8f1ff`, callout amber `#ffc857`
- **Type:** IBM Plex Mono for labels, Archivo Narrow for titles, uppercase technical lettering
- **Texture:** a fine drafting grid, sheet borders, a title block in the corner (drawn by, sheet no., scale)
- **Signature move:** parts draw themselves with dimension arrows, and "FIG. n" callouts snap onto numbered leader lines
- **Transitions:** the sheet slides out of a plan chest, and a new sheet number stamps in
- **Sound:** pencil scratch, a ruler click, and a soft analogue synth
- **Watch out:** thin white lines vanish at 1080p after compression. Keep strokes at least 2.5px.

## Reference kit (open only what you need)
- `frames/f1-f4.webp`: title, feature, stat and end card. Use them for composition and ornament.
- `DESIGN.md`: Stitch's full token set. Open it only when you need an exact colour role or type size.
- `ref/f1-f4.html`: Tailwind reference markup. Lift the ornament (SVG, borders, textures), not the page layout.

## Audit notes
- Stitch frames load: Archivo Narrow, Space Mono. Use the **Type** line above; it is the intended pairing.
- 16:9 fit at 1920x1080: f1 is 1920x1108; f2 is 1920x1108; f3 is 1920x1108; f4 is 1920x1108. Recompose these for 16:9; never scale them down to fit.
- Sanitised on import: 1x no real government office; 3x no real government office.
- Every word in the Stitch frames is placeholder copy: NEXUS, nexus.example, 99.999%, 840 ns, SOC 2, "certified", "granted". Replace all of it with the project's own facts.
