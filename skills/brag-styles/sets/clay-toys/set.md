# Clay Toys

Claymorphism: soft, inflated shapes that squash when they land, like toys pressed out of modelling clay.

- **Asked for as:** claymorphism, clay, 3D soft UI, squishy, toy-like
- **Best for:** consumer apps, onboarding, education, kids and family products, friendly fintech. Weak for enterprise and finance buyers
- **Palette:** cloud `#EEF0FF`, ink `#2B2A4C`, clay lilac `#B8A9FF`, peach `#FFB8A1`, mint `#9FE3C5`, butter `#FFE08A`
- **Type:** Bricolage Grotesque 800 for headlines, Inter for labels; dark ink on pastel only
- **Texture:** inflated shapes: radius 40 px or more, no borders; each carries a soft drop shadow plus an inner top highlight and an inner bottom shade (`inset` shadows), so it reads as moulded
- **Signature move:** squash and stretch. Things plop in (flatten on landing, spring back), a clay button dents under the cursor, and the count is balls dropping into a tray
- **Transitions:** a clay ball swells to fill the frame and the next scene is inside it
- **Sound:** pops and soft thuds over the `pulse` bed at 104 BPM; cues `pop`, `drop`, `success`
- **Watch out:** pastel on pastel fails contrast: keep text ink-dark. Squash on impact only, never as a constant wobble
