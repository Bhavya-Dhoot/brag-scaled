# Wabi-Sabi

Imperfect, quiet and handmade: rice paper, one brush stroke, and a crack repaired with gold.

- **Asked for as:** wabi-sabi, Japanese minimal, zen, kintsugi, handmade calm
- **Best for:** craft, ceramics, wellness, architecture, a founder's story, calm premium brands. Too quiet for a product demo
- **Palette:** rice paper `#EDE6D8`, sumi ink `#2A2622`, moss `#6B705C`, clay `#A8643C`, kintsugi gold `#C9A227` used once
- **Type:** Instrument Serif, large, with a lot of air; Inter small and widely tracked for labels
- **Texture:** paper grain, one uneven brush stroke, asymmetry, generous empty space
- **Signature move:** kintsugi. A brush draws one slow stroke; a crack runs through a form and then fills with gold as the claim lands: what was broken now holds
- **Transitions:** ink bleeds outward from a point and clears
- **Sound:** no bed; single plucked notes and a brush: `brush`, `scribble`, `chime`
- **Watch out:** slow styles audit as slideshows: keep the brush moving and the camera pushing through every held line. No decorative kanji or borrowed calligraphy
