# Swiss Grid

Swiss design and minimalism: a visible grid, flush-left type, one red mark, and nothing that is not needed.

- **Asked for as:** Swiss design, International Style, minimalism, minimal, clean, grid-based, Helvetica look
- **Best for:** B2B, finance, data products, reports, anything that must look exact. The safest look for enterprise buyers
- **Palette:** white `#FFFFFF`, black `#0A0A0A`, one red `#E10600`; grey `#8A8A8A` for secondary text
- **Type:** Inter 700 for headlines, tight and flush left, lower case allowed; Inter 400 for text; JetBrains Mono for figures
- **Texture:** a visible 12-column grid and baseline rules that draw in, very large numerals, no shadows, no radius, no decoration
- **Signature move:** the grid does the work. Rules draw on, blocks of text snap to columns, and one red bar travels to the single figure that matters while the number counts beside it
- **Transitions:** a column wipe: content slides out along the grid and the next slides in from the adjacent column
- **Sound:** a dry `pulse` bed at 120 BPM; `tick` on each snap, `stamp` on the figure
- **Watch out:** minimal is not empty: the rules, the red marker and the count keep moving, or it audits as a slideshow. One red element at a time
