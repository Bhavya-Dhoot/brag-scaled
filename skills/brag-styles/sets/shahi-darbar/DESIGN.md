## Brand & Style

This design system translates the luminous visual lexicon of late Mughal court ateliers into a high-density, narrative-driven storyboard terminal interface. It marries seventeenth-century illuminated manuscript traditions with cinematic sequence-planning software. The aesthetic relies entirely on flat miniature perspectives, crisp polychromatic pigment fields, intricate geometric discipline, and deliberate hand-ruled borders (*khat*) instead of synthetic camera blur or modern digital gradients.

The emotional tone balances sovereign authority, curated pageantry, and clinical drafting precision. Users engage with an interface that feels like handling burnished rag paper, bound albums (*muraqqa*), and gold-illuminated marginalia, reframed as an operative production deck for cinematic scene blocking and visual direction.

Key stylistic imperatives:
- **Manuscript Framing (*Hashiya*):** Every card, preview container, and canvas panel is treated as a leaf within an imperial folio. Structures employ concentric multi-ruled hairline borders, miniature botanical scrollwork, and invented rhythmic vegetal repeats.
- **Architectural Motifs:** Cusped multi-foil arches (*jharokha*), carved lattice perforations (*jaali*), and chevron-patterned dados (*chinikhana*) structure primary modular divisions and focal viewports.
- **Pure Invented Heraldry:** Strictly eschews all sacred calligraphy, real-world dynastic insignia, and religious script. Heraldic identity is constructed solely through interlocking geometric monograms, stylized lotus rosettes, cypress silhouettes, and symmetrical floral palmettes.
- **Flat Spatial Geometry:** Avoids simulated 3D depth, photorealistic drop shadows, and soft ambient light cones. Layering is conveyed through tonal field overlay, gold leaf bordering, and precise planar nesting.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from mineral and plant pigments ground in royal ateliers: lapis lazuli, shell gold, cinnabar vermilion, crushed malachite, and wild saffron, all suspended against warm, sizing-treated ivory parchment (*wasli*).

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary — Lapis Lazuli (`#1d3f8f`):** Represents absolute structural command. Applied to active states, sequence keyframes, primary control surfaces, and dominant manuscript framing headers.
- **Secondary — Lustrous Gold Foil (`#c9a227`):** Represents ceremonial illumination. Reserved for hairline dividing rules (*jadval*), frame outlines, selected item rings, high-priority timestamps, and highlighted metadata tags.
- **Tertiary — Rich Vermilion (`#d0342c`):** Serves as an assertive editorial accent for critical storyboard cues, key action flags, directorial marks, and destructive action controls.
- **Accent — Malachite Green (`#1f7a5a`):** Employed for temporal trackers, ready states, camera track indicators, and environmental scene tags.
- **Accent — Imperial Saffron (`#f2a51a`):** Utilized for warning states, lighting annotations, intermediate camera movements, and audio track cues.
- **Neutral Ground — Ivory Parchment (`#f6ecd6`):** The primary canvas substrate. Serves as the tactile backdrop across the entire application interface.
- **Surface Elevation — Aged Vellum (`#eee1c3`):** Applied to card interiors, floating drawer panels, and nested inspector modules.
- **Ink / Text — Lampblack Umber (`#1f1a14`):** A high-pigment, near-black umber engineered for immaculate contrast against parchment backgrounds without the harshness of pure digital black.
- **Muted Ink — Ochre Sepia (`#6f624e`):** Used for non-active metadata, secondary labels, grid lines, and decorative leaf borders.

### Surface Color Logic
Surfaces are built from flat mineral fills rather than translucent alpha overlays. Nested cards sit on `#eee1c3`, bordered by `#c9a227` gold rules or `#6f624e` hairline guides, preserving the physical feel of layered, heavy-weight paper boards pasted together to form album folios.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances courtly humanistic literary gravitas with rigorous tabular readability:
- **Headlines and Editorial Display:** Set in **EB Garamond**, conveying the classic, chiseled dignity of formal manuscript headers. All top-level section titles, scene designations, and act markers leverage true small-caps styling (`font-variant: small-caps`) with deliberate character spacing.
- **Narrative Content and Descriptions:** Body text remains in **EB Garamond**, styled with balanced leading that replicates handwritten royal decrees (*farmans*), maintaining high scanning clarity across narrative screenplay prompts and camera choreography notes.
- **Operational Data and Controls:** System readouts, timecodes, focal lengths, camera angle tags, and interface buttons are set in **Work Sans**. This prevents antique illegibility in dense digital control panels while maintaining an austere, architectural balance alongside the serif headlines.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is strictly architectural, borrowing the proportional rigor of classical court courtyards and mosaic pave panels.

### Structural Framework
- **Master Grid:** 12-column symmetrical fluid grid on desktop viewports with a locked central preview stage flanking inspector panels.
- **Storyboard Folio Rail:** Horizontal scrolling keyframe strip along the lower canvas, formatted as a multi-panel narrative scroll (*patachitra* format), using strict `space-md` gaps flanked by continuous double-line borders.
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Desktop (1280px+):** Three-tier layout consisting of a left scene-tree drawer (col 1–3), central viewport stage (col 4–9), and right parameter inspector (col 10–12).
  - **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** Inspector slides into a collapsible overlay folio; central viewport spans 8 columns, storyboard rail maintains dynamic scaling.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** Collapses to a single-column stacked view with horizontal swipe for storyboard cells and drawer menus toggled via ornamental top banners.
- **Rhythmic Margins:** High-density framing margins (`margin-desktop: 2.5rem`) establish outer page borders, emphasizing the manuscript presentation style.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial layering rejects soft modern Gaussian dropshadows and pseudo-3D lighting. Depth is produced using flat heraldic overlapping, gold leaf lining, and dual-stroke frame borders.

### Layering Mechanics
1. **Canvas Ground (Base Level):** The root viewport sits on `#f6ecd6` (Ivory Parchment).
2. **Structural Panels (Level 1):** Storyboard cells, parameter drawers, and timelines sit flush on `#eee1c3` (Aged Vellum), separated from the canvas by a multi-line border: an inner 1px rule in `#c9a227` (Gold), a 2px gap revealing base ground, and an outer 1px rule in `#6f624e` (Sepia).
3. **Elevated Overlays & Modals (Level 2):** Shot detail modals, palette extractors, and floating inspectors feature an inner flat perimeter frame with a crisp, non-blurred offset rule: `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px 0px #1f1a14`. This produces the physical profile of heavy, mounted miniature cards stacked upon a drafting table.
4. **Active Selection (Level 3):** Active items are outlined with an inset 2px border in `#1d3f8f` (Lapis Lazuli), accompanied by four corner cornerpieces styled as geometric leaf brackets.

## Shapes

The geometric identity is defined by crisp, structured corners with micro-bevels and architectural profile framing:
- **Base Geometry:** Standard components use subtle rounding (`0.25rem`), retaining the firm presence of trimmed manuscript leaves.
- **Jharokha Portals:** Feature preview windows, active scene display cards, and modal dialogs adopt an ornamental multi-cusped arch header (`border-radius: 12px 12px 0 0` with SVG cusped scalloping masks).
- **Jaali Perforations:** Divider accents, progress meters, and inactive state panels make use of 45-degree geometric diamond-trellis cutout textures.
- **Corner Notches:** Primary control cards employ 45-degree chamfered inward notches on the upper corners, referencing manuscript illumination frames (*unwan*).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Sovereign Action:** Rich Lapis Lazuli (`#1d3f8f`) solid fill, crisp 1px Lustrous Gold (`#c9a227`) border, text rendered in Work Sans Semibold Small-Caps (`#f6ecd6`). Hover state transitions fill to Malachite Green (`#1f7a5a`) with an invariant gold perimeter.
- **Secondary Heraldic Action:** Transparent ground with a dual-ruled border (1px Gold with 1px nested Sepia offset), text rendered in `#1f1a14`. Hover state floods the background with Aged Vellum (`#eee1c3`).
- **Destructive/Critical Action:** Solid Vermilion (`#d0342c`) fill, 1px Lampblack (`#1f1a14`) stroke, parchment white text.

### Storyboard Cards
- Composed of an inner visual frame bordered by a miniature vegetal scroll pattern (*hashiya*).
- Header bar features a cusped arch shape holding the shot sequence index (e.g., `ACT I • SHOT 04`) in EB Garamond Small Caps.
- Footer band houses camera metadata (lens type, motion path) in Work Sans 11px uppercase alongside an invented geometric monogram denoting the scene location.

### Input Fields
- Flat Aged Vellum (`#eee1c3`) fill with an underline-dominant framing model: 2px solid Gold Foil (`#c9a227`) base rule with hairline lateral uprights.
- Active focus state draws a continuous inner boundary line in Lapis Lazuli (`#1d3f8f`) with no outer glow. Floating labels display in Work Sans Bold uppercase, colored in Ochre Sepia (`#6f624e`).

### Chips and Sequence Tags
- Compact architectural capsules styled with 45-degree chamfered edges.
- Color-coded by department: Malachite Green for Environment/VFX, Imperial Saffron for Lighting/Key, and Vermilion for Sound Design cues. Borders remain uniformly Gold Foil (`#c9a227`).

### Checkboxes and Radios
- **Checkboxes:** Square boxes with a 1px Gold border. Checked state displays a solid Lapis Lazuli fill overlaid with a geometric eight-pointed star emblem (*khatam*) instead of a generic tick mark.
- **Radio Buttons:** Diamond-shaped containers (`transform: rotate(45deg)`). Selected state renders an inner gold diamond jewel.

### Timeline Sequence Track
- The scrub rail mimics an illuminated manuscript horizontal scroll. Keyframe beads are styled as diamond seals (*muhr*). Active frame markers feature an upright cypress-tree silhouette indicator.
