# Shahi Darbar

The launch announced in the Mughal court: jewel-toned miniature painting, gilt borders, arches.

- **Best for:** premium products and Indian-market launches; anything worth announcing like an imperial *farman*
- **Palette:** lapis `#1d3f8f`, vermilion `#d0342c`, malachite `#1f7a5a`, saffron `#f2a51a`, ivory ground `#f6ecd6`, gold `#c9a227` (gold only as a foil effect, never as flat yellow)
- **Type:** Cormorant Garamond SemiBold (a serif is right here: courtly manuscript tradition) and Rozha One for Devanagari accents, if the product is bilingual
- **Texture:** *hashiya* borders with floral arabesques, flat perspective with patterned carpets, gold leaf that catches the light
- **Signature move:** each scene opens inside a cusped arch (*jharokha*) whose doors swing open, and a gold-leaf shimmer runs along the border as the claim lands
- **Transitions:** palace doors close and reopen on the next court scene
- **Sound:** tanpura drone on Sa–Pa, sitar-like Karplus-Strong plucks in Raag Yaman, tabla *theka* under the highlights, shehnai-like lead on the CTA
- **Watch out:** no human figures copied from real paintings, and no Quranic or Persian verse as ornament. Use botanical and geometric motifs only.
