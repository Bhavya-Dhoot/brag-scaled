# Soft Press

Neumorphism: every control is pushed out of, or pressed into, one soft surface.

- **Asked for as:** neumorphism, neomorphism, soft UI, embossed, extruded
- **Best for:** hardware, smart home, audio gear, control panels, calculators. Reads dated to design-aware buyers; weak for B2B
- **Palette:** one surface `#E6EAF0`, light `#FFFFFF`, shade `#B8C0CC`, ink `#2D3440`, one accent `#3D7BFF` that means on
- **Type:** Inter 700 and 400; Space Grotesk 700 for dial values
- **Texture:** raised is a light shadow up-left and a dark one down-right; pressed is the same pair `inset`. Nothing has a border or a different background
- **Signature move:** controls. A toggle sinks and lights, a dial turns to its value, a slider knob travels its groove: each press flips raised to pressed
- **Transitions:** the whole panel presses in and the next one rises
- **Sound:** no bed, or a very quiet `pulse`; `switch`, `click`, a soft `thud`
- **Watch out:** shadow alone is not enough contrast: all text in ink, and every state change also changes colour
