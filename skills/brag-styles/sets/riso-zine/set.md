# Riso Zine

The product printed as an underground zine: two spot inks, the plates slightly out of

register, grain everywhere.
- **Best for:** indie apps, creative tools, community products, anything with personality
- **Palette:** paper `#f4f1e8`, fluoro pink `#ff48b0`, federal blue `#3255a4`, overprint violet `#6b3a8c` where the two inks overlap
- **Type:** Rubik Mono One for headlines, Space Mono for body; hand-cut labels
- **Texture:** halftone dots, a grain overlay, visible plate misregistration of 3–6px
- **Signature move:** each layer arrives as a separate ink plate and slides into register, so the image "prints" in two passes
- **Transitions:** a paper-feed slide, or a halftone dissolve through the paper colour
- **Sound:** a lo-fi drum loop, with a tape-stop at each cut and the mechanical thunk of a riso drum
- **Watch out:** keep body text in one ink, or it becomes unreadable.
