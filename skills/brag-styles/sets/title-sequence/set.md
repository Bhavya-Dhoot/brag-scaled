# Title Sequence

A 1960s film title sequence: cut-paper shapes, jagged bars and a jazz score.

- **Best for:** cinematic launches, security, AI, anything with drama
- **Palette:** vermilion `#e8452c`, cream `#f3e9d2`, black `#111111`, one of mustard `#e0a526` or teal `#1f7a7a`
- **Type:** Oswald for titles set in blocks, with hand-cut, irregular baselines
- **Texture:** paper grain; shapes with slightly uneven cut edges
- **Signature move:** jagged bars slam into frame and assemble into the product's silhouette, then split apart to reveal the title
- **Transitions:** a bar wipe, or a shape that grows to fill the frame
- **Sound:** walking bass, brushed snare and a muted trumpet stab on each title
- **Watch out:** it's the style of a famous designer. Use the grammar, never copy a specific title sequence.

## Reference kit (open only what you need)
- `frames/f1-f4.webp`: title, feature, stat and end card. Use them for composition and ornament.
