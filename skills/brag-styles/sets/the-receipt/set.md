# The Receipt

The whole pitch prints out on a till receipt: features as line items, and the total is the punchline.

- **Best for:** fintech, commerce, productivity ("time saved"), anything you can itemise
- **Palette:** receipt white `#fbfaf6`, thermal black `#1e1e1e`, faded thermal grey `#8c8c8c`, a single highlighter `#fff176`
- **Type:** Space Mono or IBM Plex Mono throughout; barcode with Libre Barcode 128
- **Texture:** curling paper, a perforated tear edge, slight thermal fade toward the end of each line
- **Signature move:** the receipt feeds out line by line from a printer slot, the camera follows it down, and "TOTAL" lands on the punchline
- **Transitions:** tear-off (a zigzag cut), then a new receipt
- **Sound:** thermal printer chatter in rhythm, tear rip, register cha-ching on the total
- **Watch out:** every line must be a real claim. Invented prices are fabricated data.
